About the Service

What Is a Clipping Path?

A clipping path is a closed vector path drawn by hand around a subject in Photoshop. Everything inside stays. Everything outside is cut away. The result is a clean, isolated subject ready to be placed on any background — no rough edges, halos, or fringing from the original shot.

It is the most accurate method for background removal. Automated tools approximate; a hand-drawn path is exact. That difference matters when images are going into a professional catalog, a marketplace listing, or a print campaign where every detail is visible at full resolution.

Clipping path works best on products with hard, well-defined edges: footwear, electronics, jewelry, bags, and furniture. For subjects with fine hair, sheer fabric, or transparent elements, image masking is the right approach — we handle both and will advise which suits your images.
