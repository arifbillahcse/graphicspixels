What We Do

Our AI Image Fix Services

From correcting isolated hand or facial errors to removing background artefacts and fixing lighting inconsistencies — we handle the full range of AI generation mistakes with precision and speed.

Hand & Finger Anatomy Correction

Extra fingers, fused hands, broken knuckle structure, and incorrect wrist anatomy are the most common AI errors. We rebuild hands using the clone stamp and healing brush, matching surrounding skin tone, texture, and anatomical proportion. Complex hand poses and interlocking fingers are corrected individually to look natural and realistic.

  • Extra or malformed fingers removed
  • Fused hands separated cleanly
  • Knuckle and joint anatomy corrected
  • Skin tone and texture matched seamlessly

Text & Typography Correction

AI consistently struggles with readable text: letters are warped, words are nonsensical, and spacing is uneven. We rewrite text in Photoshop on signs, labels, packaging mockups, UI elements, and marketing graphics. Text is rendered to match the perspective, lighting, and style of the surrounding image, looking like it was there in the original AI generation.

  • Illegible text redrawn clearly
  • Warped perspective corrected
  • Spacing and alignment balanced
  • Text integrated naturally into scene

Background Artifact & Noise Removal

AI-generated backgrounds often contain noise patches, blurred edges, repeated tiles, impossible geometry, and lighting inconsistencies. We clean these artefacts using content-aware fill and manual painting, rebuilding background areas to be seamless and realistic. The correction maintains the original lighting direction and atmospheric quality of the generated image.

  • Noise and blur patches cleaned
  • Repeated or impossible geometry fixed
  • Edges sharpened and refined
  • Perspective and lighting made coherent

Lighting & Shadow Consistency

AI-generated lighting is often physically implausible: light hits multiple directions, shadows point inconsistently, and ambient lighting is unbalanced. We correct these using curves, dodge-and-burn tools, and selective shadow painting. The final lighting reads as coherent and realistic, whether the image is supposed to be lit by sunlight, studio lights, or ambient room light.

  • Light direction made consistent
  • Shadow placement corrected
  • Ambient light balanced
  • Highlights and reflections refined
