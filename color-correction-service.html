About the Service

What Is Color Correction?

Color correction is the process of adjusting white balance, exposure, hue, saturation, and contrast to bring images to a consistent, accurate colour standard. It neutralises unwanted colour casts caused by mixed lighting, corrects exposure problems, and ensures tones match across your product catalog or shoot batch.

For e-commerce, inaccurate colour is one of the leading causes of returns — customers receive a product that doesn't match the listing photo. Color correction is one of the most direct ways to reduce returns, increase buyer confidence, and maintain your brand visual standard across every platform: website, Amazon, Instagram, print, and beyond.
