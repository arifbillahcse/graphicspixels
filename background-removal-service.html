About the Service

What Is Background Removal?

Background removal is the process of isolating your subject — a product, person, or object — from its original background so it can be placed on any surface: pure white for marketplace listings, transparent PNG for web, custom brand colour for campaigns, or a lifestyle scene for advertising.

At Graphics Pixels, every background is removed by hand using the Photoshop pen tool or layer masking depending on the subject type. Hard-edged products like shoes, electronics, and jewellery use clipping path. Soft-edged subjects like hair, fur, or sheer fabric use masking. We choose the right method for each image without you needing to specify.
