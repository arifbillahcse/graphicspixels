About the Service

What Is Ghost Mannequin Editing?

Ghost mannequin editing removes the mannequin or model from apparel photographs, leaving a clean, hollow 3D shape that shows exactly how the garment fits and falls — without any visual distractions. The result looks natural and three-dimensional, building buyer confidence and reducing returns.

It is the industry standard for fashion e-commerce. Platforms like Amazon, ASOS, and independent Shopify brands use it consistently because it presents apparel in the most accurate, professional way possible. Whether you need neck joint, sleeve, or full composite mannequin work, our editors handle every garment type to the same high standard.
