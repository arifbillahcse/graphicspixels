Who We Are

Graphicspixels: Where Every Pixel Has a Purpose

At Graphicspixels, we believe that every image tells a story. Founded in 2010, we have grown from a two-person bedroom studio into a globally recognized visual production powerhouse with over 120 dedicated specialists.

Our primary expertise lies in Professional Photo Editing and Advanced Retouching — ensuring that every pixel is meticulously aligned with your brand's vision. From e-commerce product shots to high-end beauty retouching, we deliver results that convert.

With over 1 million images processed for clients across the USA, UK, Canada, and Europe, we combine creative excellence with industrial-scale delivery.

Our Story

The Evolution of Graphics Pixels

From a bold two-person vision to a globally recognised studio — here's how we got here.

2010

The Foundation

Started as a bold two-person vision in a bedroom studio, serving our first international client via referral. The mission was simple: deliver quality that speaks for itself.

2012

The First Milestone

Reached 100+ active clients and expanded to 8 team members. Word-of-mouth growth confirmed that quality and reliability were our greatest assets.

2016

Professional Expansion

Moved into our permanent Dhaka studio, establishing formal production workflows, quality control systems, and a growing team of specialists.

2017

Video Production Launch

Launched our video editing service to provide complete visual content pipelines for brands — from still images to dynamic video content.

2018

3D Innovation

Officially started our 3D modelling division, investing in high-end workstations and industry-leading software to deliver photorealistic renders.

2023 – Present

Global Powerhouse

Now a globally recognised visual studio with a 120+ member team, serving clients across the US, UK, Canada, and EU. Over 1 million images edited and counting.
